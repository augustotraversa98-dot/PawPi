shares information when you use the PawPi mobile app and related services (the "Service"). PawPi is

records, training, community, adoption, and a marketplace of pet services and products.

If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the Service.

## 2. Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. Specifically:

**Information you provide**

- **Account information** — your email address and name (or display name) when you sign up, your
  username, an optional avatar, your role (pet owner / business / shelter), and your language and
  time-zone preferences. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the basic profile
  information those services share (name and email, subject to your choices, including Apple's
  private-relay email). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes (argon2); we never store or see
  your plaintext password.
- **Pet profiles** — information you enter about your dogs: name, handle, breed, age, birthday,
  gotcha/adoption date, gender, weight, photos, life-stage, and notes. If you opt in to leaderboards,
  a **coarse area label** you choose (never your precise coordinates).
- **Health and medical content** — information you choose to record: weight and wellness logs, photo
  checks, walk logs (including duration/distance and, if you enter it, a route or location note),
  medications, vaccinations, allergies, known conditions, microchip id, spay/neuter status, your
  vet's and clinic's name/phone/email, an emergency contact's name and phone, insurance provider and
  policy number, vet notes, lab results, surgeries, documents you upload, prescriptions/refills, and
  reminders/routines. **This is sensitive information about your pet (and, where you enter them, your
  own emergency and insurance details); you provide it for your own tracking and care.**
- **Social content** — posts, captions, photos and videos, comments ("barks"), likes ("paws"),
  follows, pet friendships, forum threads and comments, events and RSVPs, and lost-and-found reports.
- **Marketplace, booking and caregiver content** — bookings, messages with service providers and
  other owners, reviews, adoption applications (including your answers to a shelter's questions),
  insurance leads/applications, prescription/refill requests, and the family members or caregivers
  you invite (and the access scope you grant them).
- **Support communications** — anything you send us when you contact support.
- **Messages from someone who found your dog**: if a person opens your dog's tag page and sends a
  message, we collect their name (optional), their phone number and/or email address and the message
  they type. To prevent abuse we also keep a scrambled (hashed) form of their internet address.
  Opening a dog's tag page or a vet link may also record the visitor's internet address, to limit
  repeated requests.
- **Visits and notes through a shared record link**: when someone uses a link you created to show
  parts of your dog's Vet Record, we keep a scrambled (hashed) form of their internet address to
  limit repeated requests, and delete it after 90 days. If they add a vet note through it, we collect
  the note and, if they are signed in to PawPi, their account and its display name.

**Information collected automatically**

- **Location** — with your permission, your device's location is used for location-based features
  (nearby places, walks, transport/pet-taxi, events, adoption listings sorted by distance, and
  lost-and-found alerts). While you drive a pet-taxi trip and choose to share it, your live location
  is shared for the duration of that trip only. You can deny or revoke location permission at any
  time in your device settings; the app continues to work with reduced location features.
- **Photos and camera** — with your permission, the app accesses your camera and photo library so
  you can add pet photos, health-check images, documents, and posts, and save the shareable daily
  frame.
- **Push notification tokens** — if you enable notifications, we store a device push token (and its
  platform, iOS/Android) so we can deliver the reminders and updates you have turned on. You can
  disable notifications in the app or your device settings, which stops delivery.
- **Device and usage data** — basic technical information needed to operate and secure the Service
  (for example, app version, device platform, and error/diagnostic information). See Section 5 for
  the anonymous usage and diagnostics data the app can send. We record the
  version of the Terms/Privacy Policy you accepted, when, and the user-agent, as a consent record.

**Payment information**

- When you pay for a service or product, payment is processed by our third-party payment processor
  (currently **MercadoPago**).
  We do **not** store full card or wallet credentials on our servers; we receive transaction status,
  amounts, currency, and references needed to fulfil your order. If you buy a physical product, we

## 5. Anonymous usage and diagnostics

To find and fix bugs and to make PawPi better, the app can send us anonymous usage and diagnostics
data. This is separate from the information you put in your account, and it is **not linked to your
account**.

**What we collect**

- which screens you open;
- which buttons and features you tap, recorded by an internal name (for example, "services tab"),
  never by anything you typed or the words shown on screen;
- progress through key steps, such as whether a form or sign-up step was viewed, completed, or
  abandoned;
- how long things take (app start-up, screens loading, and how quickly our servers respond), grouped
  into broad time ranges, and whether a request to our servers worked or failed;
- the type of technical error when something goes wrong (for example, "network error") and the
  screen it happened on, never the error message or what you were doing;
- the app version, the major version of your operating system, your platform (iOS or Android), and
  your language setting (for example, "es-AR").

**What we never collect this way**

Your name, email address, phone number, or account ID; your pets' names or IDs; anything you write
or upload (posts, comments, messages, photos, health records); your location; or any advertising
identifier. Your IP address is not stored with these events — it is only held briefly, in scrambled
(hashed) form, to block abuse.

**How it is identified**

The data is tied to two random identifiers: an *install ID*, created on your device the first time
it is needed, and a *session ID*, which changes each time you start using the app (and again after
you have been away from it for 30 minutes or more). Both are random and generated on your device.
They are not connected to your account, email, device identifiers, or advertising identifier, so we
cannot tell which person an install belongs to.

**What we use it for**

Only to fix bugs and improve the app. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do
not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not use a third-party analytics or
advertising SDK for this: the app sends the data to our own servers, and we process it with
**Grafana Cloud** (Grafana Labs), a hosting and monitoring service (see Section 8). The aggregated
statistics we chart (counts and timings) do not contain the random identifiers.

Separately, our servers send Grafana Cloud basic technical records of the requests they handle: the
type of screen or endpoint (not the full address), the result code, how long it took, and a request
reference. These records leave out your IP address, account, name, email, anything in the address
after a "?", and the content of your requests, and they are used only to detect outages and errors.

**Your control**

This is on by default, and you can turn it off at any time in **Settings → Help improve PawPi
(anonymous)**. When you switch it off, the app stops sending data, clears anything still waiting to
be sent, and deletes the install ID from your device. (It sends one last signal that you opted out,
so we can count opt-outs.) If you switch it back on later, the app starts over with a new random ID
that is not connected to the old one. Your choice is kept on your device, even if you log out.
Deleting the app may not remove the install ID on every device, so the switch is the reliable way to
remove it.

We can also switch this collection off for everyone from our servers at any time, and the app will
stop sending.

## 6. Health information disclaimer

PawPi helps you **track changes and prepare better conversations with your veterinarian. It does not
diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional veterinary care.** Health content you enter is stored
to help you organize records and notice patterns. Documents you upload to the Vet Record are also
sent to Anthropic (Claude) to read them and suggest records, which you review before anything is
added (see Section 8). Any "insights" are informational and behavioural, never diagnostic.
Prescriptions appear in the app only as records issued by a licensed veterinary provider through the
Service.

- **With people you invite** — family members or caregivers you grant access to, at the scope and for

- **Finders, your dog's family and caregivers**: what a finder sends goes to the dog's owner and to

- **What your dog's public pages show (you decide)**: if you choose Phone or Email as the way to be

  tag page only if you turn on "Show my emergency contact on the pet tag" (off by default), and on a
  full vet link only if you chose Phone or Email. Make sure that person agrees before you share
  their details.
- **With processors and infrastructure providers (subprocessors)** — see Section 8.
- **For legal reasons** — to comply with the law, enforce our Terms, or protect the rights, safety,
  and security of users and the public.

### Sharing your dog's Vet Record with a business

Only the dog's owner can give or end this access. You choose the business, and the access applies to
every active member of that business's team in PawPi, including people who join the team while it
lasts.

**Sharing by parts.** In app versions that let you choose, you pick a vet business (or share from a
booking with another pet-care business), at least one of the parts below (nothing is selected for
you) and how long: 7 days, 30 days, 1 year, or until you revoke it. This access is read only and
includes what is added to the chosen parts while it lasts. Sharing by parts again with the same
business replaces your earlier choice.

- **Medical profile** — microchip, spay/neuter status and date, your usual vet and clinic with their
  contact details, and medical notes.
- **Emergency contact** — that person's name and phone number.
- **Insurance** — insurance company and policy number.
- **Vet notes** — all notes, yours and those added by vets.
- **Vaccinations** — the vaccination records.
- **Documents** — the list of documents and their files, whatever they contain.
- **Photo checks** — health photo checks, their photos and what was noted on them.
- **Lab results** — the result entries; attached documents, such as lab reports, are under Documents.

The allergy, condition, surgery, medication, prescription and daily health log sections are not part
of these choices, although such information can appear inside a note, document or photo that you
share.

**Older full access.** Older app versions, access given before you could choose parts, and approving
everything a business asked for use one bundle that lasts until you revoke it. Its viewing access
lets the business's team see the whole medical profile (including the emergency contact and
insurance), all vet notes and vaccinations, and open a private file of your dog if they have its
exact reference (for example, one they saw earlier). It does not list your documents, photo checks or
lab results. Approving everything a business asked for can also let its team add vet notes,
prescriptions or vaccinations to your dog's record and, if it asked for this, record care sessions
such as walks or grooming.

**Inviting a vet by link.** Some app versions let you, as the dog's owner, create an invite link
for that dog and send it to your vet yourself; PawPi sends no email. It is not the same as the
public vet link mentioned above, or as a shared record link (below). The invite link expires after 7 days, works once, and you can
withdraw it while it has not been used. It is not tied to one particular business: any signed-in,
active team member of a vet business published on PawPi who has the link can see your dog's name,
your display name and the date the link expires, with no photo and nothing from the Vet Record. Send
it only to the vet you mean to invite.

Accepting the invite only sends you a request from that business to view the record. The business
sees nothing in the record unless you approve the request. You can share by parts, in app versions
that let you choose, or deny the request. If that business already has a booking for your dog, you
can also approve everything asked (the viewing access described under "Older full access" above,
which lasts until you revoke it). For each invite we store the dog, your account, when it was
created and when it expires, whether and when you withdrew it and, once used, when, by which team
member and for which business. We store a scrambled (hashed) form of the link's secret code, not the
link itself, and keep the invite record until the dog or your account is deleted.

**How it ends, and what we record.** You can revoke access at any time on the app's Data Access
screen. Access shared by parts also ends by itself when its time is up. PawPi checks the access every
time the team opens the record or a private file, so new views are refused as soon as you revoke it
or it expires. We log those reads and anything added through the access (which access, which team
member, what and when), and we keep a record of the access you gave. We notify the business's team
when you give access and when you end it.

**What ending access cannot undo.** It does not take back what the team already saw, downloaded,
copied or noted, and a prescription a vet issued stays visible to that vet business. A temporary link
to a private file opened just before can keep working for about a minute. Some files use a long web
address that works for anyone who has it, without signing in: documents and health photos saved with
earlier app versions (or with an older version still in use), and attachments sent in messages.
Ending access does not switch those addresses off.

### Sharing parts of your dog's Vet Record by link, without an account

In app versions that offer it, as the dog's owner you can create a shared record link and send it
yourself; PawPi sends nothing. **Anyone who has the link can see the parts you chose, without
signing in**, including anyone it is forwarded to. It is not tied to a person or a business. Before
creating it you choose at least one of the parts listed above under "Sharing by parts" (nothing is
selected for you), how long it lasts (7 days, 30 days or 1 year) and whether it allows adding vet
notes (off unless you turn it on), and you accept a warning that says this.

The link shows your dog's name, your display name, the date the link ends and the chosen parts,
including what is added to them while it works. The Vet notes part includes every note and the name
shown on it. A document or photo saved to a private file opens through a temporary address that
works for about a minute. Files that use a long web address, saved with earlier app versions, are
listed but are not opened through the link, although a note or other text you share may contain
addresses or details of its own.

If you allow notes, whoever has the link can add vet notes to your dog's record. Nothing can be
changed or deleted through a link. Such a note is marked as added through a shared link. If the
person was signed in to PawPi, the note shows the display name of their account and we record which
account added it; if they were not signed in, or their account has no display name, it shows as
"Anonymous". The note is part of your dog's record: you, the people who can see that record and
anyone you share the vet notes with can read it, and you can delete it. If its signed-in author
deletes their account, their name on the note is replaced by "Former member".

You can remove a link at any time in the app (from the share screen or Data Access), and it ends by
itself on its date. PawPi checks the link on every request made with it, so new views, files and
notes are refused as soon as you remove it or it ends. Removing it does not take back what someone
already saw, downloaded, copied or noted, a temporary file address opened just before can keep
working for about a minute, and notes already added stay until you delete them. We do not keep a log
of who viewed a link. For each link we store the dog, your account, the parts, whether notes are
allowed, when it was created and when it ends, and whether and when you removed it. We store a
scrambled (hashed) form of the link's secret code, not the link itself, and keep that record until
the dog or your account is deleted.

## 9. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service.
You can delete individual content within the app at any time (posts, comments, logs, pets). When you
delete your **account**, we remove the account and the data you own (your profile, pets, and the
records associated with them, including your orders and payment records in our database). Deleting
your account does not remove records that belong to someone else or to a separate business: for
example, a provider's own record of a past appointment stays with that provider (no longer linked to
your account), and a business you created is a separate listing that deleting your personal account
does not remove. We may also keep limited data where the law requires it or where it is needed to
resolve disputes or enforce our Terms, and our payment processor keeps its own payment records under
its own policy. Push tokens are removed when you disable notifications or delete your account.
Backups are rotated on a routine schedule.

Anthropic, which reads the documents you upload to the Vet Record (see Section 8), keeps content sent
through its API for a limited period under [its own retention policy](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data),
and longer where required by law or to enforce its usage policies.

For dogs that others shared with you, you can choose to delete the posts you published about them.
Health logs and vet-record entries you added are part of that dog's care history: they stay with its
owner, no longer linked to you and shown as "Former member". If you delete your account and you had
shared a dog, the dog and everything recorded about it is deleted, including what the people you
shared it with added. We tell them before it happens.

The name, phone number, email address and message sent by someone who found a dog are deleted
automatically after 90 days, from the owner's notifications and from those of the family members and
caregivers who received them (sooner for anyone who deletes their account). We keep only that a
message arrived and the dog's name. The scrambled internet address used against abuse is also
deleted after 90 days. Notifications already delivered to a phone stay there until dismissed, and
backups are rotated on a routine schedule. A finder can ask us to delete their details earlier by
writing to support.
