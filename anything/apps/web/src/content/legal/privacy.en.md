This Privacy Policy explains how Augusto Traversa ("PawPi," "we," "us") collects, uses, and
shares information when you use the PawPi mobile app and related services (the "Service"). PawPi is
an all-in-one app for dog owners: a social feed, dog profiles, health tracking, reminders, vet
records, training, community, adoption, and a marketplace of pet services and products.

If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the Service.

## 2. Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. Specifically:

**Information you provide**

- **Account information** — your email address and name (or display name) when you sign up, your
  username, an optional avatar, your role (pet owner / business / shelter), and your language and
  time-zone preferences. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the basic profile
  information those services share (name and email, subject to your choices, including Apple's
  private-relay email). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes (argon2); we never store or see
  your plaintext password.
- **Pet profiles** — information you enter about your dogs: name, handle, breed, age, birthday,
  gotcha/adoption date, gender, weight, photos, life-stage, and notes. If you opt in to leaderboards,
  a **coarse area label** you choose (never your precise coordinates).
- **Health and medical content** — information you choose to record: weight and wellness logs, photo
  checks, walk logs (including duration/distance and, if you enter it, a route or location note),
  medications, vaccinations, allergies, known conditions, microchip id, spay/neuter status, your
  vet's and clinic's name/phone/email, an emergency contact's name and phone, insurance provider and
  policy number, vet notes, lab results, surgeries, documents you upload, prescriptions/refills, and
  reminders/routines. **This is sensitive information about your pet (and, where you enter them, your
  own emergency and insurance details); you provide it for your own tracking and care.**
- **Social content** — posts, captions, photos and videos, comments ("barks"), likes ("paws"),
  follows, pet friendships, forum threads and comments, events and RSVPs, and lost-and-found reports.
- **Marketplace, booking and caregiver content** — bookings, messages with service providers and
  other owners, reviews, adoption applications (including your answers to a shelter's questions),
  insurance leads/applications, prescription/refill requests, and the family members or caregivers
  you invite (and the access scope you grant them).
- **Support communications** — anything you send us when you contact support.

**Information collected automatically**

- **Location** — with your permission, your device's location is used for location-based features
  (nearby places, walks, transport/pet-taxi, events, adoption listings sorted by distance, and
  lost-and-found alerts). While you drive a pet-taxi trip and choose to share it, your live location
  is shared for the duration of that trip only. You can deny or revoke location permission at any
  time in your device settings; the app continues to work with reduced location features.
- **Photos and camera** — with your permission, the app accesses your camera and photo library so
  you can add pet photos, health-check images, documents, and posts, and save the shareable daily
  frame.
- **Push notification tokens** — if you enable notifications, we store a device push token (and its
  platform, iOS/Android) so we can deliver the reminders and updates you have turned on. You can
  disable notifications in the app or your device settings, which stops delivery.
- **Device and usage data** — basic technical information needed to operate and secure the Service
  (for example, app version, device platform, and error/diagnostic information). We record the
  version of the Terms/Privacy Policy you accepted, when, and the user-agent, as a consent record.

**Payment information**

- When you pay for a service or product, payment is processed by our third-party payment processors
  (**MercadoPago** and/or **Binance Pay**; card processing infrastructure may involve **Stripe**).
  We do **not** store full card or wallet credentials on our servers; we receive transaction status,
  amounts, currency, and references needed to fulfil your order. If you buy a physical product, we
  store the **shipping address** you provide for fulfilment.

## 3. Legal bases for processing (where applicable)

Where data-protection law (such as the GDPR) applies, we rely on:

- **Performance of a contract** — to create your account and provide the features you request
  (feed, health tracking, bookings, messaging, caregivers, etc.).
- **Consent** — for optional device permissions (location, camera/photos, notifications), for
  sharing pet health data with a provider, and for leaderboard opt-in. You can withdraw consent at
  any time (see Section 7).
- **Legitimate interests** — to secure the Service, prevent abuse and fraud, and operate and improve
  core functionality, balanced against your rights.
- **Legal obligation** — to keep records the law requires (e.g. certain transaction records) and to
  respond to lawful requests.

## 4. How we use your information

We use the information to:

- create and manage your account and your pets' profiles;
- provide the features you use (feed, health tracking, reminders, vet records, training, community,
  adoption, and the services marketplace);
- enable bookings, payments, messaging, reviews, and caregiver sharing;
- send you the reminders and notifications you have set up or enabled (including push);
- show location-based content when you grant location permission;
- keep the Service safe: enforce our Terms, run content-moderation and blocking, detect and prevent

- comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** track you across other companies'
apps or websites for advertising. The app declares no tracking in its iOS privacy manifest.

## 5. Health information disclaimer

PawPi helps you **track changes and prepare better conversations with your veterinarian. It does not
diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional veterinary care.** Health content you enter is stored
to help you organize records and notice patterns. Any "insights" are informational and behavioural,
never diagnostic. Prescriptions appear in the app only as records issued by a licensed veterinary
provider through the Service.

## 6. How information is shared

We share information only as needed to run the Service:

- **With other users, when you choose to** — your public dog profile, posts, and other content you
  post are visible to other users according to the feature (for example, a public post is public; a
  private walk is visible only to people you invite). Lost-and-found alerts are visible to other
  users while active. Demo/seed content is excluded from all real users' feeds and discovery.
- **With service providers you transact with** — when you book, message, review, apply to adopt, or
  request a prescription/insurance quote, the relevant business receives the information needed to
  serve you. **Sharing of pet health data with a provider happens only through explicit in-app
  consent**, scoped and revocable.
- **With people you invite** — family members or caregivers you grant access to, at the scope and for
  the duration you set; you can revoke access at any time.
- **With processors and infrastructure providers (subprocessors)** — see Section 7.
- **For legal reasons** — to comply with the law, enforce our Terms, or protect the rights, safety,
  and security of users and the public.

## 8. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service.
You can delete individual content within the app at any time (posts, comments, logs, pets). When you
delete your **account**, we remove the account and the data you own (your profile, pets, and the
records associated with them), subject to limited retention where the law requires it (for example,
transaction records kept for accounting/tax) or where data is needed to resolve disputes or enforce
our Terms. Push tokens are removed when you disable notifications or delete your account. Backups are
rotated on a routine schedule.

## 9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal
information, and to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise
these rights, contact us at **augusto@pawpi.info**. We will respond within the time the applicable
law requires.

You can also:

- control camera, photo, location, and notification permissions in your device settings;
- edit or delete pets, posts, comments, logs, and other content within the app;
- revoke a caregiver's or provider's access at any time;
- delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account).

## 10. Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account from **Settings → Delete account**. This irreversibly removes
your account and the data you own (your profile, pets, and the records associated with them), subject
to the limited legal retention described in Section 8. This is separate from logging out or resetting
local app data.

## 11. Children's privacy

PawPi is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from
them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 12. International transfers

## 13. Security

We use technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including **row-level
security** so each user's data is scoped to that user (owner, or an accepted caregiver you invited),
hashed passwords, and encrypted transport. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but
we work to protect your information and continually improve our safeguards.
