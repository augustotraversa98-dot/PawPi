# PawPi Support

Need a hand with PawPi? You're in the right place. PawPi is an all-in-one app for dog
owners — a social feed, dog profiles, health tracking, reminders, vet records, training,
community, adoption, and a marketplace of pet services and products.

## Contact us

The fastest way to reach us is by email. We read every message and aim to reply within
**2 business days**.

- **Email:** **support@pawpi.info**
- When you write in, it helps to include your account email, your device and iOS version,
  and a short description (or screenshot) of what you ran into.

## Getting started

- **Create an account** — open PawPi and sign up with your email, or use Sign in with
  Apple or Google. You must be at least 16 to use PawPi.
- **Add your dog** — go to **More → My Dog** to create your dog's profile (name, breed,
  age, photo, and more). Your dog's information is linked across the app.
- **Set up reminders** — open **More → Reminders & Routines** to create feeding, walk,
  photo-check, medical-care, wellness-check, and vet-appointment routines. Due items show
  up under **Health → Today**.

## Common questions

**How do I reset my password?**
On the sign-in screen, choose "Forgot password" and follow the email instructions. If you
signed up with Apple or Google, sign in with that same provider instead of a password.

**How do I edit or delete my dog's information?**
Open the dog's profile under **More → My Dog** (social profile) or your private
**Dog Profile** to edit details. Health and medical records live under **Health → Vet
Record**. You can add, edit, or remove records there.

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to **Settings → Delete account**. This permanently removes your account and the data
you own (your profile, pets, and associated records), subject to limited legal retention.
Deleting your account is different from logging out.

**The app is asking for camera, photos, or location — why?**
PawPi asks for these only to power features you use: the camera and photo library let you
add pet photos, health-check images, and posts; location powers nearby places, walks,
events, and lost-and-found alerts. You can grant or revoke any permission at any time in
your device's Settings, and the app keeps working with reduced features.

**How do bookings and payments work?**
When you book a pet service or buy from the shop, payment is handled by our payment
processors (MercadoPago and/or Binance Pay). PawPi does not store your full card or wallet
credentials.

## A note on health features

PawPi helps you **track changes and prepare better conversations with your veterinarian.
It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional veterinary care.** The health
content you enter is stored to help you organize records and notice patterns over time.
