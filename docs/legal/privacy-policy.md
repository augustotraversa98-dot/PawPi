## 2. Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. Specifically:

**Information you provide**

- **Account information** — your email address and name (or display name) when you sign up. If you
  use Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the basic profile information those services share
  (name and email, subject to your choices, including Apple's private-relay email).
- **Pet profiles** — information you enter about your dogs: name, handle, breed, age, birthday,
  gender, weight, photos, and notes.
- **Health and medical content** — information you choose to record: weight and wellness logs, photo
  checks, medications, vaccinations, allergies, known conditions, vet notes, lab results, surgeries,
  documents you upload, and reminders/routines. This content is entered by you for your own tracking.
- **Social content** — posts, captions, photos and videos, comments ("barks"), likes ("paws"),
  follows, pet friendships, forum threads and comments, events and RSVPs, and lost-and-found reports.
- **Marketplace and booking content** — bookings, messages with service providers and other owners,
  reviews, adoption applications, insurance leads/applications, and prescription/refill requests.
- **Support communications** — anything you send us when you contact support.

**Information collected automatically**

- **Location** — with your permission, your device's precise location is used for location-based
  features (nearby places, walks, transport/pet-taxi, events, adoption listings sorted by distance,
  and lost-and-found alerts). You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your device
  settings; the app continues to work with reduced location features.
- **Photos and camera** — with your permission, the app accesses your camera and photo library so
  you can add pet photos, health-check images, and posts, and save the shareable daily frame.
- **Device and usage data** — basic technical information needed to operate and secure the Service
  (for example, app version and error/diagnostic information).

**Payment information**

- When you pay for a service or product, payment is processed by our third-party payment processors
  (**[MercadoPago]** and/or **[Binance Pay]**). We do **not** store full card or wallet credentials
  on our servers; we receive transaction status and references needed to fulfill your order.

## 3. How we use your information

We use the information to:

- create and manage your account and your pets' profiles;
- provide the features you use (feed, health tracking, reminders, vet records, training, community,
  adoption, and the services marketplace);
- enable bookings, payments, messaging, and reviews with service providers;
- send you reminders and notifications you have set up or enabled;
- show location-based content when you grant location permission;
- maintain the security and integrity of the Service and prevent abuse;
- comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** track you across other companies'
apps or websites for advertising. The app declares no tracking in its iOS privacy manifest.

## 4. Health information disclaimer

PawPi helps you **track changes and prepare better conversations with your veterinarian. It does not
diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional veterinary care.** Health content you enter is stored
to help you organize records and notice patterns. Prescriptions appear in the app only as records
issued by a licensed veterinary provider through the Service.

## 5. How information is shared

We share information only as needed to run the Service:

- **With other users, when you choose to** — your public dog profile, posts, and other content you
  post are visible to other users according to the feature (for example, a public post is public; a
  private walk is visible only to people you invite). Lost-and-found alerts are visible to other
  users while active.
- **With service providers you transact with** — when you book, message, review, apply to adopt, or
  request a prescription/insurance, the relevant business receives the information needed to serve
  you. Sharing of pet health data with a provider happens through explicit in-app consent.
- **With people you invite** — family members or caregivers you grant access to, at the scope and for
  the duration you set; you can revoke access at any time.
- **With processors and infrastructure providers** — payment processors ([MercadoPago]/[Binance Pay]),
  our database/hosting provider (**Supabase**), authentication, and similar vendors that process data
  on our behalf under contract.
- **For legal reasons** — to comply with the law, enforce our terms, or protect the rights, safety,
  and security of users and the public.

## 6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service.
You can delete content within the app, and you can delete your entire account at any time (see
Section 8). We may retain limited records where required by law (for example, transaction records).
