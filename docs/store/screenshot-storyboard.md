# PawPi — App Store screenshot storyboard (Spanish-primary)

Argentina/Spanish is the primary store, so ES is the lead set (EN built too). Nine screens,
ordered so the emotional + shareable ones land first (Apple shows screens 1–3 in search results).
Brand frame: cream background, coral paw accent, Nunito ExtraBold headline in warm brown.
No other platform (e.g. social apps) is ever named in a caption.

| # | key | Screen (capture in ES) | Caption (ES) | Caption (EN) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | profile | Perfil + momentos | El mundo de tu perro, en una sola app | Your dog's whole world, in one app |
| 2 | feed | Feed, racha 🔥 | Compartí cada momento | Share every wag |
| 3 | birthday | Cumpleaños/hito 🎉 | Cada cumpleaños, una celebración | Every birthday, a celebration |
| 4 | share | Tarjeta para compartir | Compartí sus mejores momentos | Share their best moments |
| 5 | health | Salud → Hoy | Comida, paseo y medicación, sin olvidos | Never miss a meal, walk, or med |
| 6 | vet | Historia clínica | Todo el historial veterinario, ordenado | Every vet record, organized |
| 7 | services | Servicios / reservar | Reservá veterinarios, peluqueros y más | Book trusted vets, groomers & more |
| 8 | community | Comunidad | Encontrá tu comunidad canina | Find your local dog community |
| 9 | adoption | Adopción | Encontrá un nuevo mejor amigo | Find a new best friend |

If you ship only 5: use 1, 3, 4, 5, 2 (profile → birthday → share → health → feed).
Health screens keep the non-diagnostic disclaimer visible in-frame (already rendered).
Capture + naming: see `capture-cheatsheet.md`. Build: `python3 build_shots.py`.
