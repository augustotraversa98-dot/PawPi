# 2.72 — Insurance in-app binding + payment (Wave 7)

Read `00-README.md` (the ⚡ autonomy preamble + shared conventions) first. Web + mobile + migration. Extends
the 2.54 insurance marketplace (lead-gen) into **in-app binding + payment**: an owner can buy a policy and
pay for it inside PawPi. Decided with Tats (2026-06-18): go full in-app binding + payment. Reuse the
`insurance` capability + payments (2.3); do NOT rebuild discovery/storefront/payments.

⚠️ REGULATORY NOTE for the build agent: insurance binding is regulated and varies by region. Build the
PLATFORM mechanics (policy record, application→bind→pay→active lifecycle, documents, payment) but keep the
insurer as the party-of-record: the policy is issued under the insurer's terms, PawPi facilitates. Surface
clear disclaimers + the insurer's terms link; do NOT assert PawPi underwrites. Money = strictest RLS.

CONTEXT
- 2.54 built the `insurance` capability + insurance_plans (published public read; staff write) +
  insurance_leads (owner-or-provider scoped) — v1 was lead-gen, NO binding/payment, and explicitly noted
  in-app checkout as the follow-up "reuses 2.3 if ever wanted." This is that follow-up.
- Payments (2.3) provides provider-agnostic createCheckout/handleWebhook/getStatus/refund + subscriptions
  (recurring) — insurance premiums are recurring, so model the premium as a subscription where supported,
  else a one-off/initial charge. Degrades cleanly to "payments not configured."
- No medical data flows to insurers beyond what the owner enters (2.54 rule holds) — do NOT auto-attach the
  Vet Record. Binding uses the plan + the owner-entered pet details + the owner's acceptance of terms.

GOAL
From a plan in the marketplace (2.54), an owner can apply → bind → pay → hold an active policy: choose a
plan + pet, confirm details + accept the insurer's terms/disclaimer, pay the premium via 2.3 (recurring
where supported), and get an active policy record with its documents/number, viewable in their hub. The
insurer (provider staff) sees bound policies, their status, and payment state in the dashboard. Real data +
empty states; clear non-underwriting disclaimers.
