**Status:** ready · **migration (next free number, 0083 at time of writing)** · independent · safe-parallel:

## Context
Pet owners can already follow pets (`pet_follows`, used by discover/notifications/profile), but there is no
way to follow a **business/provider**. The provider spine is `providers` + `provider_capabilities` +
`provider_staff`; the pet-owner UI is the Expo mobile app (`service/provider.jsx` provider screen). Mirror
the `pet_follows` shape and its follow/unfollow endpoint pattern (`app/api/pets/[id]/follow/route.js`) for
providers.

**Scope this to FOLLOW ONLY — do NOT build daily-moments / video here** (that's a separate, larger,
attended effort, intentionally excluded from tonight's run).

## Current issue
A pet owner viewing a business has no way to follow it, and a business has no follower count / follower
concept. This is net-new (the last piece of the original Shop/Store + business-social ask).

## Expected behavior
1. On the business/provider screen (`service/provider.jsx`), a signed-in pet owner sees a **Follow /
   Following** toggle; tapping it follows/unfollows (optimistic, persisted, reverts on error). A guest sees
   the button but is prompted to sign in.
2. The provider shows a **follower count** where it fits on the profile.
3. A pet owner can see the **list of businesses they follow** ("Following" for providers), reusing the
   existing list/search patterns from ticket 2.61 where practical.
4. EN + ES strings, neutral "tú".
